Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on page count, functionality, content readiness and feedback turnaround. We’ll confirm a realistic timeline after understanding the project.

Will the website work on mobile devices?

Responsive behaviour for common screen sizes is part of the planned build and will be checked during testing.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, redesign projects can be discussed. We’ll review the current site, goals and technical constraints before confirming scope.

Can I update the website myself?

This depends on the platform and setup. We’ll clarify editing access and any handover or training included.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

This depends on scope. We’ll confirm whether these are included, client-provided or billed separately.

Will my website rank on Google?

We can implement agreed SEO foundations, but rankings depend on many factors and cannot be guaranteed.

Can you add custom features or integrations?

Tell us what you need. We’ll assess feasibility, cost and timeline before including custom functionality.

What do you need from me to get started?

Usually business details, goals, service information, brand assets, preferred examples and any existing website or content.
